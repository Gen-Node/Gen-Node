# Gennode — Whitepaper

**A decentralized compute and data network for bio & AI.** *Genes meet nodes.*

Gennode turns idle machines into a network for science. Operators run a node — a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux, or a headless CLI (`npx @gennode/node`) — and contribute idle GPU and storage. Contribution is measured as points (uptime × capacity), and points convert to `$GENNODE` in a future airdrop. The network is built in three layers: Compute (genome analysis, protein folding, drug discovery, multi-omics, scientific simulation, and AI inference), a planned torrent-like encrypted Data layer, and Models (open models today; a purpose-built Gennode bio/AI model later). `$GENNODE` is planned as a 100% fair launch on Base with no team allocation and no pre-mine, funded by trading fees.

Read the full whitepaper at **https://docs.gennode.org/whitepaper**.
