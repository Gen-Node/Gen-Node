# Gennode — Whitepaper

**A private, torrent-like decentralized compute network for bio & DNA.** *Genes meet nodes.*

Gennode turns idle machines into a network for science. Operators run a node — a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux, or a headless CLI (`npx @gennode/node`) — and contribute idle compute. Contribution is measured as points (uptime × capacity), and points convert to `$GENNODE` in a future airdrop. The network is built in three layers: **Compute** (DNA & genome analysis, protein folding, drug discovery, disease research, and multi-omics, with AI models for genomics and protein analysis running on the network as a compute method), a planned torrent-like encrypted **Data** layer, and **Models** (open bio models today; a purpose-built Gennode bio model later). `$GENNODE` is planned as a 100% fair launch on Base with no team allocation and no pre-mine, funded by trading fees.

Read the full whitepaper at **https://docs.gennode.org/whitepaper**.
