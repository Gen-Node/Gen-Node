# Health AI

A private, judgment-free assistant for **understanding** your health — not diagnosing it.

## What it helps with
- Making sense of symptoms and what to ask your doctor
- Understanding lab results and medical terms in plain language
- Preparing for appointments

## Doctor mode
Clinicians can use Gennode to explain conditions to patients, summarize and brainstorm — faster. **The final decision and responsibility always rest with the clinician.**

## Privacy
- No logging, no profiling, no data selling
- Queries are decoupled from your identity
- Runs on Venice's private AI infrastructure today; on confidential, self-hostable nodes in the future

## Safety
Gennode flags red-flag symptoms and points you to professional or emergency care when it matters. It **does not diagnose, treat or prescribe**.

::: warning Not medical advice
Information & wellness only. Always consult a qualified clinician. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
:::
